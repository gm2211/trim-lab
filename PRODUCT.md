# Trim Lab

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Sailors learning Colgate 26 sail trim. Adjust one line, inspect the sail and
telltales, and connect that change to speed, heel, and handling. The existing
Helm, Lines, Concepts, Drills, and 3D views share one simulation state.

## Design principles

Preserve the existing restrained instrument-panel vocabulary and both themes.
Keep line colors consistent with the rigging guides. Materials should distinguish
cloth, gelcoat, metal, and rope without hiding sail shape or airflow. Controls
must remain usable beside the boat on desktop and below it on small screens.
Use familiar buttons, visible keyboard focus, text as well as color for state,
and reduced-motion support. Avoid decorative dashboards and photorealism that
obscures the training task. These principles capture the existing app and this
review's scope; they do not introduce a new brand direction.
